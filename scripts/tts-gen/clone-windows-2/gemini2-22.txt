At 7:03 a.m. on Tuesday, Dr. Eleanor Vale stepped onto Platform 4B with a canvas satchel, two overripe pears, and a note that read, "Meet me at the old clock—no, not that clock." The station announcer, speaking in a calm but faintly metallic voice, said, "The 07:05 service to St. Ives, via Northbridge, will depart from Platform 4."
